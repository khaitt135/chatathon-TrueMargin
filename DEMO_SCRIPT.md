# True Margin — 5-Minute Demo Script

Practice this twice before you go on. Use `index.html` with seed **4821** (the default).

---

### 0:00–0:30 — Problem

"Knowledge workers like Amara spend their days in back-to-back meetings and
deadlines. She has no idea two weeks out that it's about to get bad.
Generic wellness apps tell her to 'protect focus time' — but her start
time is fixed, her meetings are fixed. She can't act on that advice.
True Margin works around what she can't change."

### 0:30–1:30 — Two-Week Dashboard

*[Open index.html. Stay on Manager view, Amara Okafor, seed 4821.]*

"Here's Priya's view of Amara's next two weeks — meetings over a private
capacity band from 1 to 5. Most days look fine. Thursday the 8th is
overloaded. Let's see why."

*[Click Thu 8]*

### 1:30–2:30 — Why It's Flagged + Recommendation

"Thursday's Architecture deep dive asks for concentration 5, but Amara is
running at 3 through that block. The math doesn't work. True Margin
suggests moving it to Friday the 9th at 10:30, where capacity covers it
and everyone invited is free."

*[Click Apply — the meeting moves; Friday is selected]*

### 2:30–3:30 — The AI Moment + Privacy

"If Priya needs to tell Amara, asking without oversharing is hard. So we
use generation for exactly one thing: drafting an editable message. It
never mentions energy, sleep, or scores."

*[Point at the labelled draft — 'template draft' or 'drafted by Claude']*

"And the check-ins behind that capacity number stay private."

*[Click View as employee, then Start the morning check-in]*

"This Pulse Ritual is what Amara sees. Priya never sees these answers —
only the 1 to 5 capacity band."

### 3:30–4:00 — Close

"True Margin isn't a wellness diagnostic or a surveillance tool. It's a
two-week reality check on what's actually controllable — built to prevent
overload, not react to it."

### 4:00–5:00 — Q&A Buffer

Anticipated questions:

- **"How is this different from Clockwise or Reclaim?"** → Those tools
  pack a calendar. We match meeting demand to derived capacity, including
  days that look free but aren't actually usable.
- **"Is the delegation message really AI?"** → "The page tries a live
  Claude call and always falls back to a local template. Either way it's
  labelled on screen and editable before send."
- **"Does the manager see check-in data?"** → "No — only the employee
  sees energy, meals, breaks, demand, and confidence. The manager sees a
  1–5 capacity band plus the calendar."
- **"What's actually built vs. simulated?"** → Point to the README's
  What's Working / Simulated / Planned sections.
